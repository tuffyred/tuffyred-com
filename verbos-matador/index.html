Verbos Matador

Spanish verb conjugation trainer by Tuffyred LLC

A look inside

Overview

This app focuses on the hardest parts of learning Spanish, mastering verb conjugations. Through repetition and bribery, Verbos Matador gives you a tool to practice verb conjugations in short quizzes that grind into a fast, satisfying drill you'll actually want to open.

  • Practice exactly what you need. Pick which of the 19 tenses and moods to work on, from the everyday presente to the trickiest subjuntivo and condicional, or just focus on one at a time.
  • Attack your weak spots. A dedicated Weak Spots mode remembers the exact verbs and tenses you miss and drills them until they stick.
  • Hide the translation. Cover the meaning and train comprehension and conjugation at the same time.
  • Hear it spoken. Pronunciation for every conjugation on devices with text-to-speech.
  • Collect as you learn. Earn points for correct answers and spend them in La Tienda on 41 treasures from across the Spanish-speaking world, each with its own photo and story. The shop runs on points you earn, never real money.
  • Full reference materials: a description of each tense, how it's used and its regular endings, plus a complete conjugation chart for any verb in every tense.

MORE TO LOVE

  • Levels by frequency (A1 to C1): learn the most useful verbs first, all the way to 1,000.
  • Six app languages: English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, Italiano.
  • A key row puts ñ and the accented vowels one tap away, and answers are accepted with or without accents.
  • Latin America or Spain: one switch adds or hides vosotros, and a few words that differ between the two are handled.
  • Streaks, an optional timer, and adjustable session length.
  • Build your own verb list: exclude what you've already mastered.
  • 100% offline. No account, no sign-up, no tracking, no ads.

Paid once. Every level and every tense is open from the first launch, with no subscription and no in-app purchases.
